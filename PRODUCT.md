# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, collaborators, and engineers who land on a personal site to decide whether Segun ships real systems. They scan in seconds on a laptop or phone, looking for proof in products and open-source tools, not a résumé narrative.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Oluwasegun Olumbe, engineer and founder in London, built to get him hired: it should show the AI engineering a hiring manager is scanning for (RAG, evals, MCP servers, agents) as case studies with architecture and reasons, not as a list of claims. It presents BashBop, Brain Boost Buddy, Snap A Bird, and the open-source tools (gate, Solumbe, tieline, bouncer, aiglare) with equal weight. Success is a visitor leaving convinced he builds both products and tools, and clicking through to the work.

## Brand Personality

Light, gentle, and technical. Direct founder voice: practical stories, no fluff. The page stays calm so the work can be specific.

## Anti-references

- The original cream/paper + red accent + mono-label look
- The dark indigo and yellow, fixed-viewport look it had before the light theme
- The white and indigo light theme that followed: all-caps heavy Bricolage headings, pill chips and buttons, dot-grid hero, offset-shadow portrait, tracked accent eyebrow labels, "01 02" numbering. It read as AI-generated.
- Generic SaaS landings (identical cards, soft gradients, Inter-default typography)
- Terminal/hacker aesthetics (neon on black, green code, monospaced costume)

## Visual System

Plain index. One sans family (IBM Plex Sans) in sentence case, near-black ink on a light warm grey, and no accent colour: hierarchy comes from size, weight, and hairline rules, and links are underlined in ink. The work is listed as a table (name, what it does, links), not as cards. Case-study architecture is a captioned figure of names in boxes, one row per layer with an arrow into the next, followed by a key that says what each part does.

## Design Principles

1. **Work is the proof.** Products and tools carry the page; decoration never outruns the substance.
2. **Equal weight.** Founder products and open-source tools share the stage; neither is a footnote.
3. **Hard to scroll past.** Scale, contrast, and rhythm should stop a fast scanner.
4. **Personal without soft.** Real stories (2am calls, built for his kid) stay specific; warmth is not the visual system.
5. **Built, not templated.** The site should feel intentional and authored, never like a portfolio theme.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast. Keyboard-reachable links with visible focus. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No information conveyed by color alone.
